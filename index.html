The cold-room problem

Cold upstairs bedroom? Look up.

When attic insulation is thin or has been pushed around by wind, heat escapes through the ceiling and the rooms right below it stay cold all winter.

We'll come out, measure what's up there and show you photos, so you can see the problem for yourself. The assessment is free.

Signs you need more insulation

If any of these sound familiar, a free attic assessment will show you why.

  • Cold upstairs bedrooms or bonus rooms
  • Rooms that are hard to heat or cool
  • High heating bills
  • Ice dams or frost in the attic
  • Drafts near outlets and baseboards
  • An older home that's never been topped up

Rebates

Insulation rebates

  1. Home
  2. Rebates

Many energy-efficiency rebate and grant programs require your attic or walls to reach set insulation levels, and the programs change. We help you understand what's needed, document the work with before and after photos, and get your home to the required level.

How it works

Three steps to a rebate-ready home.

01

Check current programs

You or your energy advisor confirm which program you're applying to and what it requires.

02

ACEM assesses and documents

We measure what you have now and photograph it.

03

We install to the required level

Then you get before and after photos and an invoice for your claim.

Please note

Program availability and amounts change.

Confirm eligibility with the program provider before starting work. ACEM does not administer any rebate program.

Questions

Rebate FAQ

Do you handle the application?

We provide the documentation your application needs.

Some programs require one before and after the work. Check yours.
